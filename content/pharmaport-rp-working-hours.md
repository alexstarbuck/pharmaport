## The plan

Medika cuts her contract from 40 h by **x** hours; Pharmaport employs her for **x** hours. Mibe (8 h/week) carries on.

| Employer | Today | Planned | Legal form |
|---|---|---|---|
| Medika d.d. | 40 h | 40 − x | contract annex → part-time (*nepuno radno vrijeme*) |
| Pharmaport d.o.o. | — | x | new **regular** part-time employment contract (*ugovor o radu*) — **not** a supplementary-work contract |
| Mibe | 8 h | 8 h | supplementary-work contract (*ugovor o dodatnom radu*), unchanged |

---
## Does it work under the Labour Act? On the text, yes.

- **Two part-time contracts adding up to 40.** ZoR čl. 62(2): a worker may not work more than 40 h/week in total across several employers, *except* in the case covered by čl. 18.a. Medika + Pharmaport = 40 → within the cap.
- **Mibe on top is still allowed.** ZoR čl. 18.a(1) lets a worker do supplementary work for another employer if they are full-time with one employer, **"odnosno radi u nepunom radnom vremenu kod više matičnih poslodavaca, tako da je njegovo ukupno radno vrijeme 40 sati tjedno"** (or works part-time for several principal employers, adding up to 40 h a week). Medika and Pharmaport would then both be her *matični poslodavci* (principal employers).
- **Condition:** the two part-time contracts must add up to **exactly 40**. If Medika cut more than the Pharmaport hours, the total would fall below 40. Then the literal condition of čl. 18.a(1) would no longer be met for the Mibe arrangement, which would need restructuring.
- **The September ambiguity is now moot** (whether the 8 h cap applies per contract or per worker, čl. 18.b(4)). There is still only one supplementary-work contract.
- **The responsible person must be an employee.** Prav. NN 83/13 čl. 9(1): the wholesaler *"moraju imati zaposlenu odgovornu osobu"*. A regular employment contract satisfies this, and it's what the application asks for (čl. 64(1) t. 6).

## Formal steps this triggers

1. **Medika:** written annex reducing her hours. Separate from the ZoR čl. 101 non-compete approval, but ideally signed together.
2. **Pharmaport:** she must tell Pharmaport about her other part-time contract (Medika) when signing (ZoR čl. 62(3)).
3. **Mibe notice:** before work starts, she must notify **each** principal employer, now including Pharmaport, in writing of the Mibe contract (ZoR čl. 18.a(3)).
4. **Mibe contract:** check whether it names Medika or her full-time status. If so, it may need an annex. *Not verified — need to see the contract.*

## What decides x — and why it isn't settled

No legal text sets a minimum number of hours for the responsible person. What we do have:
- čl. 9(3): she must *personally* perform the duties and be **"stalno biti dostupna"** (constantly available). Tasks may be delegated; responsibility may not.
- čl. 10(1): enough qualified staff "ovisno o opsegu djelatnosti" (depending on the scale of the business).
- čl. 10(3): the workload must not create an unacceptable risk to product quality.

So x is a judgement that HALMED and the Ministry's pharmaceutical inspector make at inspection (čl. 65(1)). That's why it's question 2 in the HALMED letter.

**Risk — constant availability vs. a full Medika day.** During her Medika hours she is still at Medika. How "stalno dostupna" works in practice (phone, a defined response time, a back-up person for physical tasks) needs to be written into her Pharmaport job description (čl. 9(3), last two sentences) and the SOPs. Expect the inspector to ask.
