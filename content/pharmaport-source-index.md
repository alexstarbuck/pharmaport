## Key article map (quick lookup)

**Zakon o lijekovima**
- čl. 3 t. 77 — definition of wholesale (incl. unošenje/iznošenje, uvoz/izvoz)
- čl. 3 t. 87 — **odgovorna osoba za promet na veliko**: magistar farmacije or Dir. 2001/83/EZ čl. 49 qualified + min. 2 yrs relevant experience
- čl. 3 t. 92 — "izvoz" = only to third countries (intra-EU movement is not izvoz)
- čl. 3 t. 93 — definition of paralelni uvoz
- čl. 113 — only authorised / parallel-import / parallel-distribution medicines may circulate
- čl. 115 — who may do wholesale
- čl. 117 — from whom a wholesaler may buy (+ duty to verify counterparty licence)
- čl. 118 — to whom a wholesaler may supply
- čl. 119 — licence per location; minimum requirements
- čl. 120 — ongoing holder duties (recall plan, records, QMS, safety features, falsified-medicine notification, **supply obligation**)
- čl. 123 — 90-day decision; no appeal, only upravni spor
- čl. 124 — revocation
- čl. 126 — duty to notify changes
- čl. 127 — GDP certificate issued by farmaceutska inspekcija (Ministry), 90 days
- čl. 130–134 — parallel import authorisation
- čl. 131 — 15-day pre-notice to HALMED + MAH
- čl. 141 — annual turnover reporting to HALMED
- čl. 186 (NN 136/25) — supply security; (6) reporting duty for wholesalers/parallel importers
- čl. 188.a — maximum wholesale price set by HALMED

**Pravilnik GDP (NN 83/13 …)**
- čl. 9 — responsible person: must be **employed**, personally perform duties, constantly available, may delegate tasks not responsibility; full duty list
- čl. 10(3) — workload must not create unacceptable quality risk
- čl. 11 — initial + continuous GDP training
- čl. 13–17 — premises
- čl. 14 — **no own premises → contract with a licensed wholesaler that has premises**
- čl. 64(1) — the 14-item application dossier (t. 15 deleted by NN 32/21)
- čl. 64(2) — substitution of t. 8–10 by a storage/handling contract
- čl. 65(3) — inspector's opinion within 80 days
- čl. 65(5) — licence scoped by product categories

**Zakon o radu**
- čl. 18.a — dodatan rad; (3) written notice to matični poslodavac; (4) employer may demand cessation on objective grounds, esp. statutory non-compete
- čl. 18.b(4)/(5) — 8 h/week cap; 16 h if unequal schedule
- čl. 62(2) — no more than 40 h/week across employers except under čl. 18.a
- čl. 101 — **zakonska zabrana natjecanja** (statutory non-compete during employment)
- čl. 102–106 — contractual non-compete (max 2 yrs, compensation ≥ ½ avg pay, void below avg national pay)
- čl. 229 t. 10 — offence for exceeding the dodatni rad cap

**Zakon o trgovačkim društvima**
- čl. 248 / 429 — management-board non-compete
- čl. 248.a / 429.a — conflict of interest
- čl. 389(2) — minimum share capital 2.500 €
- čl. 390(1) — minimum nominal share value 10 €, multiples of 10
- čl. 411 — book of shares / list to registry court
- čl. 412(3) — **transfer of a business share requires a notarial act or notary-certified private deed**
- čl. 413 — transfer subject to company consent
- čl. 454–456 — amending the društveni ugovor (notarial form; effective on registration)
- čl. 455(1) — 3/4 majority
- čl. 457–458 — capital increase; subscription statement in notarial form
