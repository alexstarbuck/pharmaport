## What this is

The skeleton of Pharmaport's quality system: every procedure (*standardni operativni postupak*, SOP) and controlled document that a legal text requires or clearly implies. Each entry is tied to an article. **This list is application item 11** ("opis sustava kakvoće, poslovnik kakvoće … popis standardnih operativnih postupaka", Prav. NN 83/13 čl. 64(1) t. 11). The Croatian titles below are working titles for the final documents.

### Sources read in full for this list (2026-10-09)
- **Prav. NN 83/13** — the GDP regulation, all 82 articles — plus amendments **NN 19/20** (čl. 41(1) returns window → 30 days), **NN 32/21** (deletes čl. 64(1) t. 15), **NN 146/22** (deletes "certifikat analize" from čl. 25(9)).
- **EU GDP Guidelines 2013/C 343/01**, Croatian text, chapters 1–11 + glossary (`01_sources/eu/eu-gdp-smjernice-2013-C343-01-HR.txt`). They apply in Croatia via Prav. čl. 6.
- **ZoL** čl. 117, 118, 119, 120, 126, 129, 131, 141, 186(6).
- **Zakon NN 126/19** (implementing the EU safety-features / anti-falsification regulation 2016/161), čl. 1–13.
- **Prav. NN 150/25** (shortage-prevention reporting), all 16 articles.

**Not yet read** (gaps): Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/161 itself (čl. 20–22, 24 on wholesaler verification); the regulation under ZoL čl. 141(2) on annual turnover reporting; the čl. 129(2) regulation (being fetched).

### Legend
- **Basis E** = the text **expressly** demands a written procedure/programme/plan. **Basis I** = the text imposes an obligation that can't credibly be met without one (my inference — the inspector may see it differently).
- **Who does it**, if we use the storage-contract route (Prav. čl. 14): **P** = Pharmaport runs it; **C** = physically done by the contracted warehouse under its own SOPs, while Pharmaport controls it through the contract and audits; **P+C** = both need a procedure. *The C/P split is my reading. No text says which SOPs can be "by reference" to the contractor. Prav. čl. 50(1): "Davatelj ugovora snosi odgovornost za poslove koje je ugovorio" (the contract giver is responsible for what it outsources). Confirm via HALMED letter Q4.*
- **Phase:** **L** = needed for the licence application · **PI** = needed before parallel import · **129** = before *interventni uvoz*.

## Notes and flags

1. **Prav. čl. 6 cites the superseded EU guidelines.** It refers to "2013/C 68/01" (March 2013). Those were replaced by **2013/C 343/01**, which corrects errors in sections 5.5 and 6.3 and states it "zamjenjuju" (replaces) the March 2013 text (343/01, intro + ch. 11). We follow 343/01 and cite it in the quality manual. ⬜ cosmetic, but the manual should say why.
2. **Returns window — national rule governs.** Prav. čl. 41(1) al. 2 as amended by NN 19/20: returns from non-wholesaler customers are acceptable *"unutar 30 dana nakon prvobitne otpreme"*. EU GDP 6.3 gives "na primjer deset dana" only as an example. No conflict; SOP E2 uses 30 days and cites NN 19/20.
3. **Shortage reporting (C9) — scope question.** Prav. NN 150/25 čl. 3 makes reporting mandatory for wholesalers supplying the domestic outlets in čl. 8 (pharmacies etc.), for parallel importers and for parallel distributors. **A wholesaler that only buys in Croatia and dispatches to wholesalers abroad is not obviously listed.** ZoL čl. 186(6) covers wholesalers "sukladno članku 115." generally. 🟧 Two readings → add to [Open questions](pharmaport-open-questions.md). Plan to report regardless (cheap, and it fits the supply-obligation posture).
4. **Drafting error in the regulation:** čl. 66(1)–(2) refer to "članku 65. stavku 9.", but čl. 65 has only 6 paragraphs. Read as čl. 65(5). ⬜
5. **Licence scope decides several SOPs.** D11, F4 and parts of D4 exist only if we apply for cold-chain or special groups (application item 4; Prav. čl. 66). Narrow scope = fewer SOPs, a smaller inspection, fewer contractor requirements. 🟧 decision needed.
6. **Storage-contract route ≠ fewer obligations, only different ones.** EU GDP 3.2: "Ako veleprodaja izravno ne upravlja prostorima, o tome treba biti sklopljen ugovor. Za ugovorne prostore treba biti pribavljena zasebna dozvola…" — matches Prav. čl. 14. Pharmaport keeps responsibility (čl. 50(1)) and must audit the warehouse before outsourcing starts (čl. 50(2)). B10 becomes the most important SOP Pharmaport writes.

## Proposed drafting order

1. **A1 Poslovnik kakvoće + A6 popis SOP-ova** — the frame; everything hangs off it
2. **B1 Upravljanje dokumentacijom** — defines the format for every other SOP
3. **A4 opis radnog mjesta odgovorne osobe** — ties into [RP — working hours](pharmaport-rp-working-hours.md) ("stalno dostupna")
4. **B10, C1, C2, C4, E3, E4** — the core a trading wholesaler is inspected on
5. The rest, once storage route (Q9) and licence scope are decided
