Question from Zrinka & Ammar (2026-10-09): *is a director + responsible person (RP) enough, or do we need a QP — we won't have GMP, it's only trade? And what about pharmacovigilance — do we need another separate person?*

---
## 1. QP — not required

- **What a QP is in Croatian law:** *"Odgovorna osoba za puštanje serije lijeka u promet"* (the person responsible for releasing a batch onto the market) — ZoL čl. 3 t. 56.
- **Who must have one:** holders of a **manufacturing licence**. ZoL čl. 73(1): those under čl. 72(1) (manufacturers) must *"imati zaposlenu odgovornu osobu za puštanje serije lijeka u promet koja mora biti stalno dostupna"* (employ a batch-release person who is constantly available). ZoL čl. 72(3): *"Uvoznici lijekova iz trećih zemalja obvezni su imati proizvodnu dozvolu"* (importers from third countries must hold a manufacturing licence).
- **What a wholesaler needs instead:** the **responsible person for wholesale** (ZoL čl. 3 t. 87; čl. 119(2) al. 2; Prav. NN 83/13 čl. 9). That's Zrinka.
- **She still deals with QPs' output.** Before stock goes on sale she confirms the batch was released — for EU batches that includes *"postojanje potvrde potpisane od odgovorne osobe za puštanje serije lijeka u promet"* (a certificate signed by the manufacturer's QP) — Prav. čl. 31. She checks the QP's certificate; she is not a QP.

**Conditions — this stays true only while Pharmaport:**
1. **does no repackaging itself.** HALMED's parallel-import page says repackaging is done by *"veleprodaja ili ugovorni proizvođač koji su nositelji važeće proizvodne dozvole … i … GMP certifikat"* (a wholesaler or contract manufacturer holding a manufacturing licence and a GMP certificate). The application form (Prav. NN 38/20, Prilog I) asks for the repackager's manufacturing licence and GMP certificate. → Outsource repackaging; the contract manufacturer's QP covers it (PK-01 §3.1 already says so).
2. **imports nothing from outside the EU** (ZoL čl. 72(3); Prav. NN 83/13 čl. 67).

---
## 2. Is "director + RP" enough?

**No legal minimum headcount.** The tests are qualitative:
- Prav. čl. 10(1): *"ovisno o opsegu djelatnosti … dovoljan broj stručnjaka odgovarajuće struke … te drugo tehničko osoblje"* (depending on scale: enough qualified specialists plus other technical staff).
- čl. 10(3): workload must not be an unacceptable quality risk.
- čl. 9(3): the RP may delegate tasks, not responsibility, and must be constantly available.

**In practice:** director + RP works **if the warehouse is contracted** — the contracted wholesaler's staff are the "technical staff" (Q9). With its own warehouse, Pharmaport would need warehouse staff. The **absence-cover gap** remains open → HALMED letter question 2(e).

---
## 3. Pharmacovigilance (PV)

### 3a. As a wholesaler (the licence now): no separate PV person required — but reporting duties exist

No provision makes a wholesaler appoint a PV person. Its duties are to **report**:
- **ZoL čl. 145(1):** *"… nositelj odobrenja za paralelni uvoz, uvoznik te veleprodaja obvezni su o sumnjama na nuspojave lijeka, osobito o ozbiljnim i neočekivanim, pisano izvijestiti Agenciju, a u slučaju cjepiva i Hrvatski zavod za javno zdravstvo."* (Parallel-import holders, importers and wholesalers must report suspected adverse reactions, especially serious and unexpected ones, to HALMED in writing — for vaccines also to the HZJZ, the Croatian public-health institute.)
- **Prav. o farmakovigilanciji NN 83/13 (+ 145/21) čl. 6(1):** wholesalers must also report **therapeutic failure**, per GVP Module VI.
- **Same, čl. 27(1)–(3):** wholesalers must report events affecting the risk/benefit balance that don't qualify as an individual case report (ICSR), and adverse reactions in special situations: pregnancy and breastfeeding, children and the elderly, overdose, abuse, off-label use, medication errors, occupational exposure, lack of efficacy.

**Gaps:** ZoL čl. 145 gives **no deadline for wholesalers** (its 30-day deadline in (2) is for health workers). The GVP Module VI guideline the regulation points to is **not in our sources**.

→ The RP can run this through an SOP. **That SOP was missing from the master list — now added as SOP-RK-06.**

### 3b. As a parallel-import holder (phase 2): open

- **Prav. o farmakovigilanciji čl. 25:** *"Nositelj odobrenja za paralelni unos lijeka odgovoran je za farmakovigilancijske aktivnosti u sljedećim slučajevima: – ako nositelj odobrenja u Republici Hrvatskoj nema odobrenu lokalnu odgovornu osobu sukladno članku 19., 20. i 21. ovoga Pravilnika, – ako je dano odobrenje za paralelni unos lijeka sukladno Zakonu i pravilniku donesenom na temelju ovoga Zakona."* (The holder of a parallel-import authorisation is responsible for PV activities (1) if the Croatian MAH has no approved local PV person, (2) if a parallel-import authorisation has been granted.)
- **Two readings:**
  - **(i)** The second indent covers *every* parallel-import authorisation, so Pharmaport is always responsible for PV on its parallel-imported products.
  - **(ii)** The indents are read together, so responsibility arises only when the Croatian MAH has no local PV person.
  - The text says "paralelni **unos**", while the Act says "paralelni **uvoz**" — wording inconsistency.
- **What "PV activities" means for a parallel importer is not spelled out.** The heavy obligations — PV system (ZoL čl. 147), EU and Croatian PV persons (čl. 148), the PV system master file (PSMF), the local PV person approved by HALMED (Prav. čl. 19–24) — are addressed to the **"nositelj odobrenja"**, which ZoL čl. 3 t. 51 defines as the MAH. ZoL čl. 145(1) lists the MAH and the parallel-import holder separately.
- HALMED's parallel-import page, however, says EU PV rules *"i odgovornu osobu za farmakovigilanciju, odnose se i na lijekove u paralelnom opskrbnom lancu"* (including the PV responsible person, also apply to medicines in the parallel supply chain).

→ **Whether Pharmaport needs its own local PV person once it parallel-imports is not settled by the text. Ask HALMED before the first parallel-import application** (Q19).

**If one is needed:**
- **Zrinka might qualify.** ZoL čl. 3 t. 58 accepts a *magistar farmacije* with *"dvije godine radnog iskustva u struci s odgovarajućim dokumentiranim obrazovanjem iz područja farmakovigilancije"* (two years in the profession plus documented PV training; topics in Prav. čl. 21(1)).
  - **But** a non-physician must have a doctor available (Prav. čl. 19(4)) and be a 24-hour contact for HALMED (čl. 20(1)).
  - Combined with the RP role, it would add to the čl. 10(3) workload question.
- **Outsourcing is allowed:** a contracted local PV person employed by a legal entity registered for PV services (Prav. čl. 19(3)).
